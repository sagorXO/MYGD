## 1. Commercial Structure & Settlement Terms

1.1 **Fixed Price Engagement:** The total professional fee for the complete architecture, engineering, integration, hardware deployment, and comprehensive 12-month support of the **MY GERMAN DÖNER Operations Control Suite** is fixed at **€12,000.00 (Twelve Thousand Euros)**.

1.3 **Payment Settlement Options:** All contract payments, including the initial advance deposit and subsequent monthly installments, are **payable via SEPA Electronic Bank Transfer or Direct Cash Remittance against an Official Written & Signed Payment Voucher / Receipt**.

1.4 **Cyprus Statutory Dual-Rate Value Added Tax (VAT):** All figures are quoted net of Value Added Tax. In accordance with the **Cyprus Value Added Tax Law (Law 95(I)/2000 as amended)**:
- Statutory VAT shall be added to invoices where applicable under Cyprus commercial rules.
- The platform calculates statutory dual rates: **9% Reduced VAT** for food, dine-in, takeaway, and non-alcoholic drinks ($\text{Net} = \frac{\text{Gross}}{1.09}$), and **19% Standard VAT** strictly for alcoholic beverages ($\text{Net} = \frac{\text{Gross}}{1.19}$).

## 3. Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Maintenance Terms

3.1 **Maintenance Scope:** Included within the 12-month contract value is dedicated engineering maintenance and support provided by the Contractor:
- Local Windows PC server daemon (`192.168.1.50`) and Cloud Linux VPS monitoring.
- Security patch management and database backup verification.
- Shopify API version compatibility updates.
- Defect resolution for reproducible bugs and software non-conformities.

3.2 **Target Uptime:** The Contractor targets **99.9% system availability** for cloud-hosted backend APIs and digital signage streaming services.

3.3 **Incident Severity & Guaranteed Response Times:**

| Severity Level | Definition | Response Time | Target Resolution |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|
| **Severity 1 (Critical Outage)** | Entire system down, till unable to process sales, or local server offline. | **< 1 Hour** | Immediate triage / Emergency patch within 4 hours |
| **Severity 2 (Major Impairment)** | A core module is impaired (e.g. Menu Board screen dark, print spool fail), but till continues. | **< 4 Hours** | Same-day resolution / Workaround within 12 hours |
| **Severity 3 (Minor / Cosmetic)** | Minor UI layout glitch, typo in build sheet, non-blocking administrative issue. | **< 24 Hours** | Next scheduled maintenance release |
