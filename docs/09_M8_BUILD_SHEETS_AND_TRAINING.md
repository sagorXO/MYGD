# Module M8: Training SOPs & Visual Build Sheets
## Functional & Technical Specification (`docs/09_M8_BUILD_SHEETS_AND_TRAINING.md`)

> **Replaces:** On-the-job training by observation only and inconsistent sandwich portion sizes.  
> **Target Device:** Staff Tablet (`/staff`) and Line Displays.  
> **Key Value:** McDonald's-style visual photo assembly build sheets for every menu item, linked directly to the master recipe database (M11).

---

## 1. Visual Step-by-Step Build Sheets

Staff can tap any product on the `/staff` tablet to view high-resolution visual build sequences:

### Example: Original German Döner Build SOP
```
[ Step 1: Bread Toasting ] ➔ Toast Turkish Fladenbrot in press for 45 seconds until crispy.
[ Step 2: Base Sauce ]     ➔ Apply 1 generous stroke of Knoblauch (Garlic) sauce to bottom bread.
[ Step 3: Salad Foundation ]➔ 40g crisp red cabbage & lettuce blend.
[ Step 4: Sliced Meat ]     ➔ 150g freshly sliced rotisserie meat (checked on kitchen scale).
[ Step 5: Secondary Sauce ] ➔ 1 stroke Kräuter (German Herb) + Scharf (Chili) if requested.
[ Step 6: Fresh Toppings ]  ➔ 3 fresh tomato slices, cucumber ribbons, and fresh chopped parsley.
[ Step 7: Final Wrap ]      ➔ Slide into branded greaseproof MYGD triangle paper sleeve.
```

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) Library

- **Meat Skewer Mounting & Heating SOP:** Step-by-step instructions for placing raw skewers, testing core temperature, and carving technique.
- **Sauce Preparation SOP:** Batch recipes and mixing instructions for homemade Kräuter, Knoblauch, Scharf, and Feta-Olive sauces.
- **Hygiene & Knife Sharpening Standards:** Visual guides for daily sanitization and blade maintenance.
